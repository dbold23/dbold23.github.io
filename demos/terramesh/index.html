Gaia

A planetary data interpreter

Plain, deterministic statistics on saved records. Not a chatbot: the same records always give the same numbers. For every place and species it answers three questions.

  1. 1What do the records say?
  2. 2How sure are we?
  3. 3What would one more visit settle?

Interpretation makes asks. Asks make visits. Visits change the interpretation.

Review

Only people make a record count

The phone suggests a name. It never counts as a find. A record reaches research grade when two or more people review it and more than two thirds agree.

Privacy

Tiles, not pins

  • Public maps show coarse hexagonal tiles, never exact points
  • Sensitive species and private places never appear in public
  • Faces are blurred before anything is shared
  • Sound is kept only around detections, and speech never
  • New features ship switched off, and turn on only after a field test proves them

How it is built

Phone, station, outward

  1. Phone

    SwiftUI app with ARKit and LiDAR capture, on-device species suggestions (BioCLIP, Perch, BirdNET) and counted effort for every survey.

  2. Station

    An append-only evidence vault. COLMAP photogrammetry with ARKit priors, sound ID, individual shark re-ID (MiewID, LightGlue) and face blurring.

  3. Outward

    Reviewed records in Darwin Core with effort and absence, on H3 tiles, through Cloudflare Workers, built for GBIF and public place pages.
