Distance Model Parameters

                        
VHF tag signal propagation model based on path loss formula.

                        
Typical VHF collar: 5-15 dBm

                        
RTL-SDR stock antenna: ~2 dBi

                        
Free space: 2.0 | Forest: 3.5-4.0
